Desperate times call for desperate measures.

I had deliberately kept the Root node out of the network's day-to-day operations — it's the server that knows everything and can do everything, which is exactly why I'd been guarding it like something I couldn't afford to lose. The less visible it is, the less likely it is to get noticed.

But people need internet access right now. Not later, not someday — today. So I had to unholster it.

Network connectivity is restored. Everything is running normally. I'll connect the bot and other services later — the network is up already.
