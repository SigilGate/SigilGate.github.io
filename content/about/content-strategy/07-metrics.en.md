## Goals (for linking metrics)

- **G1:** Trust, brand recognition, author and project expertise.
- **G2:** Client acquisition, project growth and scaling.
- **G3:** Contributor attraction.

G1 and G2 are directly linked: growing trust and expertise generates a flow of clients. G3 is a supporting goal whose relevance increases as the project matures.

---

## Collection Process

Metrics are collected monthly. For each indicator, one of the following value types is recorded:

- **Abs.** — absolute value at the end of the month (subscribers, stars, members).
- **Total** — cumulative total for the month (published posts, client inquiries, PRs).
- **Avg.** — average value for the month (views per post, ER per post, read-throughs).

Comparing indicators month over month allows tracking the project's development dynamics and evaluating results.
