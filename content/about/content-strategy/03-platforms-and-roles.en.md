{{< lead >}}
Content is distributed across six platforms. Each serves its own purpose and addresses its own audience.
{{< /lead >}}

---

## Website (sigilgate.github.io)

**Role: source of truth.**

The central hub of the project. This is where the blog, technical documentation, and project page live. All materials are published on the website first — external platforms receive adapted versions, but the website always remains the primary source. It serves as a showcase, an archive, and a knowledge base.

**Formats:** long-form articles, architectural documentation, research, guides.

---

## Habr

**Role: technical megaphone.**

The platform for reaching the Russian-speaking technical audience. Deep technical materials go here — architectural deep dives, protocol analysis, real-world cases. Habr provides access to engineers and infrastructure specialists who might not otherwise learn about the project.

**Formats:** adapted versions of technical articles from the website, with links to the original source and Telegram channel.

---

## Zen

**Role: broad reach.**

The platform for materials that go beyond pure technology: reflections on freedom of information, digital sovereignty, the impact of technology on society, and the role of AI. Zen allows us to reach an audience that doesn't read Habr but cares about these topics.

**Formats:** adapted versions of philosophical and overview articles from the website.

---

## Medium

**Role: English-language presence.**

Reaching the international audience. Medium is a natural platform for English-language publications on privacy, network infrastructure, and internet freedom. It helps attract specialists and readers from outside the Russian-speaking community.

**Formats:** English-language versions of key materials.

---

## Telegram (@SigilGate)

**Role: uncensored community platform.**

A space for open discussion without the censorship and restrictions typical of other platforms. The channel is for publications, announcements, and editorial notes. Groups are for live dialogue, experience sharing, and discussing topics that are difficult to raise on public platforms.

**Formats:** short posts, announcements, behind-the-scenes insights, open discussions in groups.

---

## Discord

**Role: technical core.**

The platform for interaction with developers and contributors. Technical discussions take place here, project work is coordinated, and code and infrastructure questions are resolved. Discord attracts the international technical community and provides a structured space for collaboration.

**Formats:** topic-based channels, discussions, task coordination.
