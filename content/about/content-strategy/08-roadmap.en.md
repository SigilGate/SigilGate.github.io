{{< lead >}}
Content strategy development is tied to the project's technical development. No specific timelines are set — the project evolves organically, and the transition between phases is determined by product readiness and accumulated material.
{{< /lead >}}

---

## Phase 1: Launch

**Project technical stage:** MVP development, prototyping.

**Focus:** regular content production, material accumulation, building a publishing habit. The blog functions as a working diary — documenting the product creation process, research, architectural decisions, and reflections.

**Channels:**
- Blog (RU + EN) — primary channel.
- Telegram — adapted versions of selected posts, automatic news publications.
- Word of mouth — manual link sharing.

**Primary KPIs (from the [metrics catalog](../07-metrics)):**
- Number of published posts (production metric) — regularity and volume of accumulated material.
- Telegram subscriber count (G1) — initial audience growth.
- Telegram post views (G1) — initial response.

**Condition for transitioning to the next phase:** a sufficient volume of material has been accumulated for a series of articles on external platforms. The project is technically ready for pilot operation.

---

## Phase 2: Expansion

**Project technical stage:** pilot operation, technical maturity.

**Focus:** launching on external platforms. Accumulated material is reworked into a series of articles and published on media platforms — in a concentrated manner to ensure publication regularity and reach a critical mass of coverage and subscribers (target — several hundred subscribers on external platforms).

**Channels:**
- Blog and Telegram — continue operating in Phase 1 mode.
- Habr — technical articles.
- Zen — philosophical and societal content.
- Medium — English-language versions of key articles.
- Telegram — warm-up posts are added: announcements, topic discussions, polls.

**Primary KPIs (from the [metrics catalog](../07-metrics)):**
- Subscribers on external platforms: Habr, Zen, Medium (G1) — absolute value and growth.
- Views and read-throughs of articles on external platforms (G1) — reach and content quality.
- CTR of links to blog and Telegram (G1) — effectiveness of driving traffic to owned channels.
- Telegram ER (G1) — engagement growth with the introduction of interactive formats.
- Number of published articles on external platforms (production metric).

**Condition for transitioning to the next phase:** a sustainable audience has been established on external platforms. The project is technically ready for production operation and scaling.

---

## Phase 3: Growth

**Project technical stage:** production operation, scaling.

**Focus:** organic audience growth, deepening community engagement, client acquisition. Growth rates depend on the demand and popularity of publications. All distribution channels are operating at full capacity.

**Channels:** all channels are active, including Discord as a working platform for clients and developers.

**Primary KPIs (from the [metrics catalog](../07-metrics)):**
- ER across all platforms (G1) — depth of audience engagement.
- Project mentions in external blogs, podcasts, channels (G1) — organic spread.
- Direct client inquiries (G2) — content-to-business conversion.
- Search engine rankings (G1, G2) — long-term visibility.
- Issues and PRs from external contributors (G3) — if open source components exist.
- Discord member count (G2, G3) — growth of the working community.
